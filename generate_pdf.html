NyayaKavach (न्यायकवच)

Secure Digital Document Management System for Legal & Investigation Documents

PROBLEM STATEMENT ID: PS-SIH26190 • MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS

1. Executive Summary & Problem Alignment

Law enforcement agencies, courts, forensic labs, and legal departments process massive volumes of sensitive documents (FIRs, Charge Sheets, Witness Statements, Forensic Reports, Evidence Records, Judgments). The NyayaKavach system digitizes, encrypts, and immutably anchors all documents to a zero-trust blockchain audit ledger, resolving data fragmentation, tampering risks, and compliance friction.

4. Data Lifecycle & Workflow Steps

    Ingestion & OCR: Mobile/Web upload $\rightarrow$ PaddleOCR extracts metadata (FIR No, BNS Secs) $\rightarrow$ PII redaction engine masks sensitive witness details. Encryption & Vault Storage: File encrypted with AES-256-GCM envelope encryption and saved to sovereign MinIO cloud vault. Blockchain Anchoring: SHA-256 cryptographic digest + Officer ID + Timestamp anchored to Hyperledger Fabric transaction log. Zero-Trust Sharing: ABAC permissions share file across Police, FSL, Prosecution, and Judiciary with live watermarking. Judicial Evidence Admissibility: Magistrate verifies hash integrity; system auto-generates BSA 2023 Sec 63/65B Certificate.
